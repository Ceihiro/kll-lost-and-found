How It Works

Lost or found something on campus? Here's the simple step-by-step process.

1

Lost something? Post it in a minute

Tell us what you lost, its color, and where you last had it (like Lab 2, Room 101, or the Hallway). No account or sign-up needed—just fill out the quick form and you're done.

2

Found an item? Help a classmate out

Picked up an umbrella, tumbler, calculator, or student ID? Log it here, then surrender it to the campus guard or guidance office. We keep private details hidden so only the real owner can claim it.

3

Automatic matching & instant results

Our smart system checks your report against all found items immediately. It compares item type, color, campus location, and descriptions—even if written in Taglish or student shorthand.

4

Head to the office & claim it

See a match that looks like yours? Go to the office. Simply describe a private detail only you know (like your wallpaper, specific stickers, or scratches) to verify ownership and take it home!

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the KLL Lost and Found system.

How does the AI know which found item matches my lost report?

The system uses a Hybrid AI matching engine. It compares your report against all logged found items using two methods at the same time: (1) rule-based attribute matching — category, color, brand, location, and time window; and (2) NLP text similarity using TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity. The final score is a 50/50 combination. The Top 3 most likely matches are shown to you with an explanation of why they matched.

Can I report my lost item in Tagalog or Taglish?

Yes! The system is designed to understand common Tagalog and Taglish descriptions. Words like payong, calcu, tumbler, itim, pula, may sticker, and popular Filipino brands like AquaFlask and Bavin are all recognized and matched correctly. You can also write in English — both work.

How do I claim a found item that matches mine?

Go to the matches page for your lost report. If a found item looks like yours, bring yourself to the campus office or the guard who found it. A staff member will ask you to describe a private detail about the item — for example, what is on your lock screen, a specific sticker, or a hidden mark. This is kept secret from the public to prevent false claims. If you correctly describe it, the staff member marks the claim as Verified and you get your item back.

What happens if nobody claims a found item after 30 days?

Found items that remain unclaimed for more than 30 days appear in the staff dashboard's overdue reminders list. The staff can then decide to mark the item as Unclaimed, which removes it from the public listing. The item record is never deleted — it remains in the system audit log for reference.

Is my personal contact information visible to the public?

No. Contact details, private item descriptions (e.g., a hidden identifying mark), and storage location are never shown on the public pages. They are only visible to authorized staff who are logged in. The public listing only shows general information like category, color, and general location.
