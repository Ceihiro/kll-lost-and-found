How It Works

Lost or found something on campus? Here's the simple step-by-step process.

1

Lost something? Post it in a minute

Tell us what you lost, its color, and where you last had it (like Room 101, or the Hallway). No account or sign-up needed—just fill out the quick form and you're done.

2

Found an item? Help a classmate out

Picked up an umbrella, tumbler, calculator, or student ID? Log it here, then surrender it to the office. We keep private details hidden so only the real owner can claim it.

3

Automatic matching & instant results

Our smart system checks your report against all found items immediately. It compares item type, color, campus location, and descriptions—even if written in Taglish or student shorthand.

4

Head to the office & claim it

See a match that looks like yours? Go to the office. Simply describe a private detail only you know (like your wallpaper, specific stickers, or scratches) to verify ownership and take it!

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the KLL Lost and Found system.

How does the system match my lost item with found items?

When you submit a report, our system automatically compares it against all logged found items in two ways:

  • Item Details: It checks if the category, color, brand, location, and date line up.
  • Word & Text Similarity: It reads what you wrote in your description and calculates word overlap with found reports.
  • Top 3 Recommendations: It calculates a combined match score and shows you the 3 most likely items, saving you from searching through every single post.
Can I write my report in Tagalog or Taglish?

Yes, definitely! Feel free to write naturally in English, Tagalog, or a mix of both:

  • Everyday Filipino campus terms like payong, calcu, tumbler, itim, pula, or may sticker are recognized automatically.
  • Popular student brands (such as AquaFlask, Casio, or Bavin) are detected even with common abbreviations or quick shorthand.
How do I claim an item that matches mine?

Recovering your item is quick and safe for everyone:

  1. Check Your Results: View your AI matches to see which found item looks like yours.
  2. Visit the Office: Head over to the Campus Guard post or the faculty office where the item is kept.
  3. Provide Your Secret Detail: Staff will ask you to describe a private detail that only the true owner would know (such as your phone lockscreen wallpaper, stickers on the back, or internal scratches). This detail is kept hidden from public view to prevent wrong claims.
  4. Claim Your Property: Once verified, staff logs the confirmation and hands your item back to you.
What happens to items that nobody claims after 30 days?

If a found item sits unclaimed for more than a month:

  • It appears in the staff dashboard's Overdue List so campus administrators can follow up.
  • Staff can mark it as Unclaimed to keep the public list clean and focused on recent items.
  • The record is never deleted from the system, so if you return later to look for it, staff can still look up where it is stored.
Is my student ID or contact information visible to anyone?

No, your personal privacy is completely protected:

  • The public website only shows basic item details (such as the item type, color, description, and where it was found).
  • Your name, student ID, course/section, and contact details are strictly hidden from students and can only be seen by authorized staff when logged into the portal.
